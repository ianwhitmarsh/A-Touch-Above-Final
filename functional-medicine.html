What Is Functional Medicine?

Your body is connected. Your care should be too.

Functional medicine is an integrated, metabolic approach that looks at the whole person to identify patterns that are out of balance and the root causes behind how you feel.

Our team blends evidence-based medicine with functional and holistic care — and the faith-centered compassion A Touch Above is known for — so you understand what's happening in your body and have practical steps to feel better.

Symptom-focused care

  • Asks "what" is wrong
  • Short visits
  • One-size-fits-all
  • Manages symptoms

Functional medicine

  • Asks "why" it's happening
  • Time to hear your story
  • Personalized to your biology
  • Addresses root causes
