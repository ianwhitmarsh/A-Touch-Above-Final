Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2025 · Last Updated: August 7, 2025

At A Touch Above Holistic Health, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website, book services, or communicate with our team. No mobile details or personally identifiable information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Your name, email, phone number, and other contact details when you book an appointment or fill out a contact form.
  • Health Information: Any medical or wellness details you provide through intake forms or during your sessions. This includes information shared in-person, via our booking platform, or through secure communication.
  • Payment Information: When booking online, we may collect payment details (e.g., credit card) via a secure third-party processor. We do not store full payment information.
  • Website Usage Data: We may collect non-personal data such as browser type, device information, and site usage patterns to improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Personalize services and wellness plans
  • Communicate with you about your care
  • Send occasional updates or promotions (only with your permission)
  • Improve our website and services
  • Maintain records in compliance with professional and legal requirements

3. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal and health information. This includes secure platforms for scheduling, encrypted data transmission where applicable, and restricted access to sensitive information.

If you use a Health Savings Account (HSA) or Flexible Spending Account (FSA), we process this through secure, compliant channels.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health information. Your data is only shared:

  • With your explicit consent
  • With third-party providers who help us operate our website or services (such as booking software or payment processors) under strict privacy agreements
  • When required by law (e.g., for health regulations or legal obligations)

5. Your Rights

  • Access the personal or health information we hold about you
  • Request corrections or updates to your information
  • Request deletion of your data (except where records must be retained for legal or clinical reasons)
  • Withdraw consent for communication at any time

To request access or changes, please contact us at the email or phone number listed below.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies to improve user experience and track general site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. We may also use basic analytics tools to better understand website traffic and performance.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
