Owner & Founder

Tara Safran

B.C.D.F.M., A.I.M.O.S., C.H.H.P., N.C.B.T.M.B.

With more than 30 years of hands-on experience, Tara has built A Touch Above around one belief: the body has a remarkable ability to heal when it's truly supported. A near-death experience at 19 set her on this path, and as a 10-year breast cancer survivor she understands firsthand the challenges patients and families face throughout a cancer diagnosis.

Her care is faith-based, and she specializes in functional medicine, terrain-based health, metabolic wellness and integrative oncology support. Tara is a wife, a mother of three and a grandmother.

Family Nurse Practitioner

Kimberly Martin

N.P., APRN, FNP-C

Kim brings 16+ years of healthcare experience, including critical access hospitals and Level I trauma centers. Her journey into integrative health became deeply personal after the heartbreaking loss of her 5-year-old son to leukemia — an experience that shaped her passion for supporting the whole person throughout their health journey.

Kim is the founder of MTM Wellness and has advanced training through the Metabolic Terrain Institute of Health. She blends evidence-based and functional medicine with a root-cause approach, helping clients connect conventional medical care with nutrition, lifestyle, metabolic health and whole-person wellness. She is a proud mother of four.

Neuromuscular Therapist

Adrienne Hills

CIMT · IANMT Certified

Adrienne is a Neuromuscular Therapist with certifications from both the Colorado Institute of Massage Therapy and the International Association of Neuromuscular Therapies. She specializes in Craniosacral Therapy, Prenatal Massage, Trigger Point Therapy, Integrative Techniques and cupping. With extensive knowledge of the human body, she blends a variety of modalities to create a customized massage experience tailored to each person’s individual needs and goals. Her approach is centered around pain relief, restoring balance, and supporting the body’s natural ability to heal.

Understanding that the body can carry the effects of past experiences and trauma within the musculoskeletal system, Adrienne takes a trauma-informed approach to her work. She is committed to creating a safe and respectful environment, where each person feels comfortable, heard and supported. During the 2020 pandemic, Adrienne became deeply passionate about health and overall wellness, which ultimately led her to massage therapy and the ancient wisdom of healing touch. Along that journey she came to embrace a simple but powerful belief: people need people. We are not meant to move through life disconnected from one another, and sometimes healing begins simply by feeling safe enough to be seen, heard, and cared for. This belief became the foundation for her work, and it guides her in the way she approaches each person who trusts her with their body and story.

Outside of the clinic, Adrienne is a mother to three beautiful children, an avid gardener, and a lover of movement. She finds peace and joy through running, dancing and nurturing her family, as well as tending to her small homestead. She believes wellness extends beyond the massage table — it is found in connection, movement, nature, family, and the everyday moments that bring us back to ourselves.
